Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: November 10, 2026

Agla Consulting LLC wants everyone to be able to use aglaconsulting.com, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, voice control or keyboard-only navigation.

Our goal

We work to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content easier to use for people with disabilities, and they make the site better for everyone.

What we've done

  • Built the site with standard, semantic HTML: headings, landmarks, lists and labeled form fields.
  • Provided text alternatives for meaningful images and hidden decorative icons from screen readers.
  • Made all links, buttons, the mobile menu and the contact form usable with a keyboard, with visible focus indicators.
  • Checked text and background colors against WCAG contrast guidelines.
  • Announced form success and error messages to screen readers.
  • Designed pages to work on phones, tablets and desktops, and to support browser zoom up to 200%.
  • Reduced motion for visitors who turn on "reduce motion" in their device settings.

Known limitations

Accessibility is ongoing work, and some areas may not fully meet the standard yet. Content and tools provided by third parties, such as LinkedIn, Google services and embedded forms, are outside our direct control. If you run into a barrier, please tell us and we'll work to fix it or provide the information another way.

How we assess the site

This statement is based on a self-assessment using automated checks and manual review, including keyboard testing. It isn't a third-party audit. We review the site when we make significant changes.
