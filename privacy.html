1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, work email, phone number (optional), the service you're interested in, and the message you write.
  • Email, phone and meetings: anything you choose to share when you email or call us, book a consultation, or meet with us, such as your business name, role and how your business handles leads, quotes and billing.
  • Business relationship information: if you become a client, the contact, billing and contract details needed to deliver and invoice our services.

Please don't send us sensitive personal information (such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers or health information) through the contact form or email.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device information: pages you view, links you click, how you arrived at the Site (for example, a search engine or LinkedIn), approximate location based on your IP address, browser and device type, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Lead and click events: we record when the contact form is submitted successfully and when someone clicks our phone number, email address or LinkedIn link. These events include the service selected on the form and where on the page the link was clicked. They do not include your name, email, phone number or message.
  • Server logs: our website host records basic technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the Site.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule your free consultation and follow up about our services.
  • To provide, manage and invoice services for our clients.
  • To understand how the Site is used and which pages and channels bring people to us, so we can improve the Site and our marketing.
  • To measure the results of our advertising, if we run ads.
  • To protect the Site, prevent spam and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the information you send us through the Site to train artificial intelligence (AI) models.

3. How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for them to provide their services:

  • Formspree, which receives contact form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • Google (Google Workspace for email and documents; Google Analytics for Site analytics; Google Ads for advertising measurement, if we run ads).
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the Site.
  • Other business tools we use to communicate with you, schedule meetings, and manage proposals, contracts and invoices.

We may also share information if required by law, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, sale or reorganization of our business. In that case the information would remain subject to the promises in this policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses Google Analytics 4, through the Google tag, which uses cookies and similar technologies to measure visits and interactions. Google processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy. You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Our Google Analytics account is linked to Google Ads, so we may use aggregated Site data to measure and improve our advertising. You can control this in several ways:

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Manage personalized ads at Google My Ad Center.

The Site links to third-party sites, such as LinkedIn. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

5. Client data in our engagements

When we design or manage automation for a client, we may access information in that client's systems (for example, their CRM, email inbox, forms and the details of their own customers). In those engagements:

  • We act on the client's behalf and use that information only to deliver the services the client asked for, under our written agreement with that client.
  • The client keeps ownership of its data, accounts and workflows.
  • We don't sell client data or use it for our own marketing. Any case study we publish is anonymized and only with the client's permission.

If you are a customer of one of our clients, please contact that business directly with privacy questions about your information. We will help them respond.

6. Calls, email and text messages

  • If you contact us, we'll reply by email or phone. You can ask us to stop following up at any time.
  • We don't send marketing text messages without your consent. If you agree to receive texts from us, you can reply STOP to opt out at any time. Message and data rates may apply.
  • If we send marketing emails, each one will include a way to unsubscribe.

7. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and contact information for as long as needed to respond and maintain a business relationship with you, and then delete it, generally within three years after our last contact unless you ask us to delete it sooner. We keep client contract and billing records for as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes. Google Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings in our Analytics account.

8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on the Site, access controls and multi-factor authentication on our business accounts, and limiting access to people who need it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal or contractual reasons.
  • Stop sending you marketing communications.

To make a request, email sales@aglaconsulting.com with the subject "Privacy request." We may need to verify your identity before acting on it, and we aim to respond within 45 days. We won't treat you differently for making a request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, and we will honor them as required.

10. Children

The Site and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our business and tools change. We'll post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above. If we make significant changes, we'll make reasonable efforts to let clients know.
