The Lead-to-Cash Lifecycle, Automated

Lead-to-Cash (L2C) covers every step from first inquiry to money in the bank. Most small businesses lose revenue in the handoffs between stages. We close those gaps with AI and automation.

Stage 1

Capture

Lead Management
Where revenue leaks

Slow replies, inquiries buried in inboxes and messy CRM records. Most buyers go with whoever responds first.

What we automate

AI agents take in website forms, social leads, chats and emails 24/7, check and clean each lead, reply in minutes, and log it in your CRM with its source.

We measure

Speed to lead: time from first inquiry to first response.

Learn more about AI lead capture and inbound lead agents
Stage 2

Qualify

Lead Conversion & Qualification
Where revenue leaks

Time spent on poor-fit inquiries while good leads go cold waiting for a callback.

What we automate

AI asks your qualifying questions, scores the lead, converts it to an opportunity, and routes it or books the appointment.

We measure

Lead-to-opportunity rate: share of inquiries that become qualified opportunities.

Stage 3

Quote

CPQ: Configure, Price, Quote
Where revenue leaks

Quotes take days, pricing varies by who wrote it, and nobody follows up.

What we automate

Your pricing and discount rules generate a professional proposal in minutes, with automatic follow-ups until you get a yes or no.

We measure

Quote turnaround: time from qualified opportunity to quote delivered.

Learn more about quote and proposal automation
Stage 4

Close

Contract & Order Management
Where revenue leaks

Approved quotes stall waiting on signatures, deposits or scheduling.

What we automate

E-signature and deposit requests go out automatically, and accepted quotes become scheduled jobs or orders.

We measure

Quote win rate: share of quotes that turn into signed work.

Learn more about closing and e-signature automation
Stage 5

Collect

Billing & Collections
Where revenue leaks

Invoices go out late and someone spends hours chasing payments.

What we automate

Completed work triggers the invoice and a payment link, reminders run on their own, and payments sync to your accounting.

We measure

Days to cash: time from completed work to payment received.

Learn more about invoicing and payment collection

One connected system, not five disconnected tools. Each stage hands off to the next automatically, so nothing waits on a sticky note, an inbox or a busy owner.

You don't have to automate everything at once. We start at the stage costing you the most, whether that's the full lifecycle, just quoting or just billing. We baseline these five numbers before we begin and report them after go-live, so you can see where conversion and cash flow improved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about Lead-to-Cash automation, AI and working with Agla.

What is Lead-to-Cash (L2C) automation?

Lead-to-Cash is every step from a customer's first inquiry to payment in your bank account: capturing the lead, qualifying it, sending a quote, closing the deal, and invoicing and collecting payment. L2C automation uses AI and connected software so each step happens quickly and hands off to the next without manual work.

Do I need to replace my CRM or other software?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use, such as your CRM, calendar, e-signature, invoicing and accounting software. If you don't have a CRM yet, we help you pick one that fits the size and budget of your business.

Will AI talk to my customers, and how do I stay in control?

AI agents can take in website, social media, chat and email inquiries, check and complete each lead, reply in minutes, ask qualifying questions and send quotes and reminders. You decide what they're allowed to do. Quotes and customer messages can require your approval until you trust the system.

How long does it take to get started?

Most engagements start with a fixed-scope pilot on the single stage costing you the most, typically measured in weeks rather than months. We expand to the rest of the lifecycle once the pilot proves its value.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is fixed-scope with the outcome, timeline and price agreed up front. The consultation is free, and you'll get a clear recommendation and quote afterward.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small and midsize businesses that rely on inbound leads and quotes, including home services and trades, professional services, medical and dental offices, real estate and other local service businesses.

Do you work outside the St. Louis area?

Yes. We're based in the St. Louis area and work on-site with businesses within about 100 miles, including St. Louis City and County, St. Charles County, Jefferson County, Franklin County and the Illinois Metro East. We also work remotely with businesses nationwide.

Who owns the data and the systems you build?

You do. Your data, accounts and workflows stay in your name, and we provide documentation so you're never locked in.
