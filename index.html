Inbound Lead-to-Cash Automation

Our flagship offering: we improve, automate and optimize the path from a new inquiry to a paid invoice for small and midsize businesses.

1. Capture & Qualify

AI agents answer calls, texts and web inquiries around the clock, gather the details you need to price the work, and route every lead to the right place so none go cold.

2. Quote & Book

Your pricing and scheduling rules produce a professional quote in minutes. Follow-ups run automatically, accepted work lands on your calendar, and edge cases are flagged for your review.

3. Invoice & Get Paid

Completed work triggers invoicing, payment reminders and CRM updates, so revenue is collected faster with less manual paperwork.
